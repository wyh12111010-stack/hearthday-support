Privacy policy

Hearthday · v1.0.0 · 2026-10-20

Where your data is stored

Study tags and timers, recipes, multi-day menus, ingredient filters and checks, cooking progress, checklists, training records, plans, collections, room layouts, preferences and feedback drafts are stored on your device. This version has no app account, uploads none of this data to developer servers, and has no ad tracking or cloud sync. System device backups depend on your system settings.

Permissions & reminders

Cooking reminders use local notifications and request permission when needed. Refusing doesn't stop timers; permission can be revoked in system settings. Lock-screen previews may show recipe or step names; turn previews off in notification settings. The home currently uses built-in weather and your device’s local time. It does not retrieve real weather or device location. This version does not read Apple Health, contacts, camera or microphone.

Export, restore & clear

In Backup & data, export JSON or CSV, validate and restore backups, or clear local data for free. JSON contains all exportable data; CSV is a readable record only. Save clipboard copies safely and back up before clearing. Uninstalling may delete local data.

Feedback & external links

Feedback drafts stay on your device. Copying feedback or opening email requires your action. Task records, health records, screenshots and logs are not attached automatically. External websites handle visits under their own rules. Review emails before sending.

Illustrations & interactions

Character illustrations are bundled with the app. Tapping triggers preset contextual replies. Your content is not sent to generative AI services for these interactions.

Membership and purchases

When membership is available, Apple App Store processes subscriptions and payments, and StoreKit verifies transactions. The developer does not receive your Apple account password or card details. No separate app account is required. Backups cannot grant membership. Reports are statistical snapshots stored on your device and included in full backups and local data clearing.
